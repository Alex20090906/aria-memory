# Hugging Face: proposed uploads (nothing uploaded)

Proposals only. Each needs the owner's approval and an account before anything is uploaded. Licence for the
datasets and the reflex model: Apache-2.0, as the repository. Text generated by Qwen3.8-Flash-Next inside the
datasets (the cached listening output) falls under its licence terms, the Qwen Community License 1.0.

| # | Name (proposed) | Type | Size | Content | Privacy check |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `aria-memory-zh-household-21d` | dataset | ~0.7 MB (text) | Two 21-day Taiwanese-Mandarin household scripts (`set_old`, `set2`): every spoken line with speaker role and timing, 90 facts, 72 questions each (51 told, 21 never told or similar) with expected answers, the speech-recognition transcripts of the synthetic audio, and the research thinker's cached listening output. Same files as `tests/fixtures/set_old`, `tests/fixtures/set2`. | Synthetic: scripts written by Gemini, voices are Windows TTS, names invented. Renamed per the project's naming rule (docs/REVIEW.md). | 2 | `aria-memory-alwayson-21d` | dataset | ~0.3 MB (gzip) | The 21-day always-on timeline (6,114 events: owner speech, TV, family, visitors, appliances, simulated camera captions) as heard by speech recognition, and the reflex model's decision on every tick. Same as `tests/fixtures/alwayson/`. | Synthetic; decompressed and scanned, 0 hits. |
| 3 | `aria-reflex-gru` | model | 0.9 MB (numpy) or 3 PyTorch checkpoints | The always-on reflex model: Linear(1029→128)+GELU, GRU(128), 5 actions plus an "encode" head; 231,686 parameters; trained on the synthetic Chinese world v6. Not proposed for now.** |
